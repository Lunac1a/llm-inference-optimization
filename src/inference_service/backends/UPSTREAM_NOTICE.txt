Modifications: disjoint historical-cache/current-BF16 loads, history-only scale
loads, current K/V pointer parameters, masked page-table tail reads, and
constrained wrapper validation.
The online softmax, causal mask and GQA mapping remain upstream. Prefill query
tiling is enlarged to BLOCK_M=64; small-query Decode geometry remains unchanged.
This module does not implement quantization, page allocation or decode routing.

Single-loop tile-source branching loads only historical FP8/scales or current
BF16 for whole-source tiles; boundary tiles retain masked dual-source reads.
The dot/softmax/accumulator body is shared. The prior three-loop split
experiment is preserved in the local research archive, not shipped.
